**Visually probe** the behavior of **trained machine learning models** , with minimal coding. [Get started](/what-if-tool/get-started) A key challenge in developing and deploying responsible Machine Learning (ML) systems is understanding their performance across a wide range of inputs. Using WIT, you can test performance in hypothetical situations, analyze the importance of different data features, and visualize model behavior across multiple models and subsets of input data, and for different ML fairness metrics.
